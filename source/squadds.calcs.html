squadds.calcs.coupled_transmons module#

Two transmons coupled to each other, each read out through a half-wave resonator: exact design from Hamiltonian targets.

Targets (GHz, f = omega / 2 pi) are the parameters of the Hamiltonian

H = sum_q H_transmon(E_J^q, E_C^q) + sum_r f_r c_r^+ c_r + K n_A n_B + sum_qr G_qr n_q (c_r + c_r^+) + R x_A x_B

f_q and eta of each exact transmon (charge basis), the bare f_r, J = K n01_A n01_B with the exact charge matrix elements, and chi = half the dispersive shift computed from the full Hamiltonian (6 transmon levels in their exact eigenbasis with every charge matrix element, 5 photons per resonator, counter-rotating terms kept; no rotating-wave approximation). The circuit side maps a Maxwell matrix (fF; ground, cross A, cross B, claw A, claw B) plus lambda/2 lines at the claws to E_C, K, G and R through the full inverse of the node capacitance matrix.

Used by the MCP tool solve_coupled_transmon_hamiltonian. Only numpy and scipy are needed.

squadds.calcs.floating_transmon module#

Transmon Hamiltonians computed from the full Maxwell capacitance matrix of a planar qubit.

Rows carry positive magnitudes <a>_to_<b> (fF): the self terms <a>_to_<a> are the Maxwell diagonal and the mutuals are \(|C_{ab}|\). The Maxwell matrix of the present conductors is rebuilt from them (ground excluded, every conductor that is not part of the qubit mode held at ground: coupler pads are loaded by their resonators, whose capacitance is much larger than the qubit’s).

  • FloatingTransmonHamiltonian: two islands (TransmonPocket), the pad-to-pad (differential) mode.

  • XmonHamiltonian: one grounded island (Xmon) with any number of claws, \(C_\Sigma\) = Maxwell diagonal.

Both reuse the TransmonCross transmon approximations: \(E_J\) from the target \((f_q, \alpha)\) via Transmon.find_EJ_EC, \(f_q\) and \(\alpha\) of each row from scqubits with that \(E_J\) and the row’s \(E_C\), and \(g\) from TransmonCrossHamiltonian.g_from_cap_matrix on the equivalent grounded two-node circuit.

squadds.calcs.floating_transmon.floating_transmon_capacitances(C, names, pads=('pad_top', 'pad_bot'), coupler=None)[source]#

Effective capacitance of the pad-to-pad mode and its effective coupling capacitance to one coupler (fF).

With every other conductor at ground, the two-pad block of the Maxwell matrix is \(\begin{pmatrix} C_{11} & -C_{12} \\ -C_{12} & C_{22} \end{pmatrix}\), \(C_{i\Sigma} = C_{ii} - C_{12}\) (pad \(i\) to ground plus every grounded coupler pad). The mode \(\varphi = V_1 - V_2\) (with \(Q_1 + Q_2 = 0\)) has

\(C_\mathrm{eff} = \frac{1}{(C^{-1})_{11} + (C^{-1})_{22} - 2 (C^{-1})_{12}} = C_{12} + \frac{C_{1\Sigma} C_{2\Sigma}}{C_{1\Sigma} + C_{2\Sigma}},\)

and a coupler \(k\) at voltage \(V_k\) drives it through the capacitive division

\(C_{g,\mathrm{eff}} = \frac{C_{2\Sigma} C_{1k} - C_{1\Sigma} C_{2k}}{C_{1\Sigma} + C_{2\Sigma}}, \qquad \beta = C_{g,\mathrm{eff}} / C_\mathrm{eff}.\)

Returns (C_eff, |C_g_eff|); the second value is 0 when coupler is None or absent.

Return type:

tuple[float, float]

g_from_cap_matrix(C, C_c, EJ, f_r, res_type, Z0=50)[source]#

Calculate the coupling strength ‘g’ between a transmon qubit and a resonator based on the capacitance matrix formalism.

Uses the formula:

g = (C_g / sqrt(C_Q + C_g)) * sqrt(hbar * omega_r * e^2 / det(C)) * (E_J / (8 * E_C,Q))^(1/4)

where det(C) = (C_Q + C_g)(C_r + C_g) - C_g^2 is the determinant of the capacitance matrix:

C = [[C_Q + C_g, -C_g ],

[ -C_g, C_r + C_g]]

Parameters:

  • C (-) – Qubit self-capacitance to ground, C_Q (in femtofarads, fF).

  • C_c (-) – Coupling capacitance between qubit and resonator, C_g (in femtofarads, fF).

  • EJ (-) – Josephson energy of the qubit (in GHz).

  • f_r (-) – Resonator frequency (in GHz).

  • res_type (-) – Type of resonator. Can be ‘half’ or ‘quarter’.

  • Z0 (-) – Characteristic impedance of the transmission line (in ohms). Default is 50 ohms.

Returns:

Coupling strength ‘g’ between the qubit and the resonator (in MHz).

Return type:

  • g (float)

Uses the formula:

g = (C_g / sqrt(C_Q + C_g)) * sqrt(hbar * omega_r * e^2 / det(C)) * (E_J / (8 * E_C,Q))^(1/4)

where det(C) = (C_Q + C_g)(C_r + C_g) - C_g^2

Parameters:

  • C (-) – Qubit self-capacitance to ground, C_Q (in fF).

  • C_c (-) – Coupling capacitance, C_g (in fF).

  • EJ (-) – Josephson energy (in GHz).

  • f_r (-) – Resonator frequency (in GHz).

  • res_type (-) – ‘half’ or ‘quarter’ wave resonator.

  • Z0 (-) – Characteristic impedance (ohms). Default 50.

Returns:

Coupling strength in MHz.

Return type:

  • g (float)
