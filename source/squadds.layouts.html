squadds.layouts.capacitance_graph module#

Layout embedding planar-capacitance-graph-v1: the geometric vectors the planar-capacitance models read.

One vector per conductor (60 shape and context features; ground is conductor 0), one per conductor pair (42 features: 17 geometric pair features, the ground-pair flag, 9 local-context ring fractions and 15 ground-block features) and one global vector (4: log chip area, log pocket area, log substrate thickness, effective permittivity). Exact embedding (the paper model’s; no far-sample decimation). Needs the [ml] extra.

from squadds.layouts.capacitance_graph import encode emb = encode(“chip.gds”) emb[“node_features”].shape, emb[“pair_features”].shape, emb[“global_features”].shape # (N, 60), (N(N-1)/2, 42), (4,)

squadds.layouts.dataset_export module#

GDS export in the convention of the planar capacitance dataset (SQuADDS/SQuADDS_PlanarCapacitance).

The networks were trained on this export, so a component exported here gives the model the input it was trained on. Layers: ground with its etched pocket on (1, 0), one polygon per signal body on (1, 10), 2 um port stubs on (2, 0) / (3, 0); 1 nm database unit. The chip is the component extent plus m ground gaps on each side:

  • helpers convention (squadds2-helpers generate_gds.py; interdigital / paddle exp6-9, concentric exp10, star exp11-12): interdigital chip = fingers + 2 m max(gap_ground) with m = 20, component centred; concentric chip = 2 R_ground (+ CPW lengths in y) + 2 m min_trace_gap, star 2 R_pocket (+ cpw_length) + 2 m gap, m = 8; the Metal GDS renderer output, bodies merged per conductor; star layouts post-processed by squadds.components.export_fixups (export_style).

  • families convention (exp13-15 generator): chip = qgeometry extent + 2 m g, m = 8 (Xmon: g = max(cross_gap, ground_spacing); shielded concentric: g = ground_gap) and m = 4 for TransmonPocket (g = the pad-to-pocket gap, floored at 20 um); nets unioned from qgeometry, renderer ground, a port stub at the readout pin and end slots at the others.

export_dataset_gds rebuilds the component from its class and options in a fresh design at the origin (orientation 0) and reproduces the dataset GDS bit-exactly (scripts/planar_capacitance/verify_component_gds.py), except where the ground gap was set from unrounded generator parameters (Xmon, TransmonPocket: pass ground_gap_um) and for the star styles under another GEOS version (see export_fixups).

squadds.layouts.qmetal_gds module#

Faithful Qiskit Metal GDS export with explicit two-terminal port markers.

Qiskit Metal stores component geometry in millimetres. SQuADDS layout GDS files use micrometres and reserve (1, 0) for a ground plane with one etched hole, (1, 10) for signal metal, and (2, 0) / (3, 0) for the ordered terminals. The helpers here keep those concerns in one place so generated datasets and their validation use exactly the same geometry contract.

squadds.layouts.qmetal_gds.GROUND_DOMAIN_PADDING_UM = 169.0#

Per-side ground-plane margin in micrometres.

Measured from 120 published GeneralizedCapNInterdigital files, whose padding is essentially uncorrelated with conductor width (Pearson -0.10), so that sweep uses a fixed absolute margin rather than a multiple of the device. A proportional rule would place the ground plane furthest from the largest device, which is the opposite of what an absolutely anchored coupling spectrum needs. 169 um is the pooled median of the per-side paddings.

squadds.layouts.two_transmon module#

A two-Xmon layout: two Xmon crosses with a claw each, coupled across a gap (“gap”) or through an interdigital capacitor (“interdigital”), and the ordering of its Maxwell matrix (ground, cross A, cross B, claw A, claw B).

The geometry vector is p = [L_A, L_B, claw fraction A, claw fraction B, coupling] (um; claw fraction 1 = the claw runs along the whole arm up to 15 um from the perpendicular arms; coupling = the gap in “gap”, the capacitor’s lead length in “interdigital”). Fixed parameters are FIXED / IDC. Used by the MCP tool generate_two_transmon_gds.

Validity: this layout is not a family of the planar capacitance dataset; ground_spacing = 0 and the 11.7 um claw lead are outside the trained Xmon ranges.
